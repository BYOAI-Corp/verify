    What a result means
    
    
    VERIFIED (signed receipts only: case handoffs, Verified Declaration receipts, release seals) means the receipt has not been changed since it was signed, nothing is missing from it, and it was signed by the key you gave. For a case handoff with source files, each source marked VERIFIED is byte for byte the file the trail names.
    
    
    CHAIN INTACT — NOT SIGNED (ticket ledgers, call logs, gate logs, custody exports) means the rows fit together, nothing more. These files carry no signature: anyone can build a chain like this. Rely on one only together with a signed seal or anchor from someone you trust. Without its anchor, a missing last row cannot be seen.
    
    
    NOT VERIFIED or NOT INTACT means at least one check failed; the reasons are listed. Do not rely on the receipt.
    
    
    What it does not prove: who the signer is as a person (a receipt says ID_UNCHECKED unless an identity check was done) and exactly when it was made (time is self-asserted). It proves the record is intact and which key signed it.
